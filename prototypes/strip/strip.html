Strip

The other form, chosen in Settings in place of the notch: the bezel grown into a 36pt strip on the screen's edge that never shuts, every session a mark on it in the panel's order. It grows as long as its marks need, centred where the notch sits, up to the screen's height (Length: Grows), or runs the full height (Full). A mark under the pointer pours its panel row out of the strip as a tab.

Try: rest on a mark, then run up and down the strip with its tab out · flick across it and nothing opens · click a mark before its tab is out · open TR's tab, fitting under the top end's S · send a new ask with a tab out · end a turn, and it moves up into needs you at once · answer an ask, and it moves down into working at once · Too many, and hover the count, its tab fitting into both ends · Many asks, past what needs you's places hold, and they fold into the orange count, then a new ask lands in it and an answer comes out of it · Remove, Few and None, and watch it shrink, down to the notch at rest, then a new ask to grow it back · drag it along the edge.

  1. dwell 120ms on a mark, reaching 3px
  2. pour its row out, flared into the strip, filleted into its S at the ends
  3. glide to the next mark, the words crossing in 160ms
  4. grace 300ms, 60ms fast
  5. draw back in, through a dent
  6. jump a mark or its tab: press, spin 450ms
  7. arrive at once, the ring flashing 2×
  8. grow to its marks, or shrink, the ends leading out and following in
  9. settle 1000ms after leaving, the order within a group

A mark opens its tab on hover; a click on the mark jumps, as a click on its tab does, even before the tab is out: then the ring presses in and spins. An away mark's click does nothing, and a count's opens its tab at once. The strip's places are Max, or what the full height holds; needs you, its asks then its stopped (blue), takes them first, all over the hairline, and past its room the end of its order, the stopped and the away first, folds into its own count at the end of its group, orange while it holds any ask and blue holding only the stopped; the grey count takes the last place for working that doesn't fit. A new ask past needs you's room takes its place and the last stopped mark showing goes into the count; with none showing it lands in the count, which flashes its ring, with Pulse for a new ask on; off, it moves in unflashed. A turn ending moves its session into needs you at once, as a new ask does, its ring flashing only with Pulse when Claude stops on. The strip never takes the keyboard, so a click leaves the typing where it was. The strip's ends are the board's S, at the top under the menu bar and at the bottom mirrored back into the bezel, so it reads as a tall notch; they never change. The first mark sits Room further under the top's S, and the last keeps Room clear of the bottom's, so a tab there fits into the strip's shape: its edge runs back into the strip and meets the S's curve in a fillet, under its bump. Length, Grows, centres the strip where the notch sits on the edge and makes it as long as its marks need, up to the full strip, or Max places long if that is shorter; its ends lead the marks out as it grows and follow them in as it shrinks (Lead), and a press on it dragged along the edge carries it. With nothing running it draws back into the notch at rest, and hovering that says so. Full runs it down the screen's whole height. The menu bar is see-through, as macOS 26's is. Off the strip and its tab, the tab draws back after the grace; a click anywhere off them and the controls draws it back at once. The bezel stands in for the screen's edge: a pointer in it, or pushed past the stage's side, counts as at the edge, where the Mac's cursor stops.
